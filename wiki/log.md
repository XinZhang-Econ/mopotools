**Operation**: Ingest of comprehensive Riksbank evaluation (2015-2024)

**Source processed**:

**Pages created**:
- qe-sweden.md - Comprehensive page on Swedish monetary policy, negative rates, and QE (2015-2024)

**Pages updated with Sweden-specific findings**:
- effects-on-yields.md - Added Sweden pass-through evidence
- effects-on-gdp.md - Added Sweden QE effects and conventional policy estimates
- effects-on-inflation.md - Added Sweden inflation effects and exchange rate channel findings
- negative-interest-rates.md - Added Sweden negative rate experience and diminishing returns evidence
- transmission-channels.md - Added Sweden transmission analysis (exchange rate and cash flow channels)
- forward-guidance.md - Added Sweden forward guidance effectiveness
- research-debates.md - Added Sweden case study and methodological comparison
- index.md - Added qe-sweden to country studies, updated source count

**Key findings from this source**:
- Sweden QE (2015-2019): ~1% GDP boost, +20-50 bps inflation (Kolasa, Laséen & Lindé 2025)
- Sweden Covid-era QE (2020-2023): ~0.2% GDP, +0.25 pp inflation - weaker than pre-Covid
- Negative rates effective initially but diminishing returns below -0.25%
- Exchange rate channel dominant for small, open economy
- Balance sheet consequences: SEK 25 billion capital injection required in 2024
- Coordinated wage-setting (Industrial Agreement) dampened inflation persistence
